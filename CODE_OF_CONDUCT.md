# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Give and accept actionable technical feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing sensitive information.

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized
content, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban participants for behavior they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's contact link
on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/digitarald). Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
